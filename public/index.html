Good to Know 💜

🛡️ PG-13, always

Chatverse is a kid-friendly zone. Messages pass through a safety filter, and characters are instructed to keep every reply clean and kind. If something slips through, steer the chat back to fun — or pick another character.

💬 How chatting works

Tap any character to chat 1-on-1. Everyone shares one community AI brain, so please be cool: there's a daily chat limit per person so the fun stays free for all. Your chats are saved on this device only.
